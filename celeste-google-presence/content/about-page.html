About Celeste L'Amour

Celeste L'Amour is a United States direct-to-consumer activewear brand that makes sculpting leggings, shorts and supportive bras engineered for tummy control, lift and all-day comfort. The company was founded in [TODO: founding year] and is based in Chicago, Illinois. Celeste L'Amour is a trading name of VIRTULIFT LLC and sells directly to customers through celestelamour.com.

The name

Celeste means of the sky — the hour after a practice ends. L'Amour means love. The name is the whole brief: something you love enough to keep wearing after class.

What we make

Every Celeste L'Amour style starts from the same problem. Most sculpting leggings solve for one thing and sacrifice another — they hold you in but dig at the waist, or they feel soft but go sheer the moment you squat. We build for both at once.

  • Sculpting leggings. High-waisted, scrunch-back, with four-way stretch and a squat-proof knit. Tummy control that smooths without pinching.
  • Shorts. The same waistband and knit in a shorter length, for warm weather and higher-intensity training.
  • Bras. Seamless, wireless, light support, cut to layer under everything else in the range.

Sizing and fit

Celeste L'Amour is sized XS through XXL. Full body measurements for every size are on the size chart. If you are between sizes, size down for a compressive fit and up for an easy one.

Shipping and returns

Orders are processed in one to three business days. Standard shipping within the United States takes three to five business days and is complimentary on orders of $75 or more. We accept returns for 30 days from delivery on unworn items with tags attached, and we provide the return shipping label. Full terms are in our shipping policy and refund policy.
